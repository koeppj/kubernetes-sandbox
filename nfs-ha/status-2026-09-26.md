## Storage incident on `ubuntu-slave1`

At **01:18:13–01:18:23 UTC**, kernel logs show failed ATA writes and an
`I/O error, dev sdb` during the `nfs` initial resync. DRBD then logged
`Local IO failed ... Detaching`, bitmap metadata I/O errors, and
`Failed -> Diskless`. At 14:03 UTC, further read errors from `/dev/sdb` were
logged during read-only inspection. The physical device reported as `/dev/sdb`
is the 1.5 TB `WDC WD15EARS-00Z` disk with serial `WD-WMAVU1286378` at this
observation; use the serial and stable device identity rather than relying on
the `/dev/sd*` letter for maintenance.

The device-mapper paths for `nfs-vg/nfs-lv`, `nfs-vg/drbd-nfs`, and
`nfs-vg/drbd-nfs-state` still exist, but `pvs`/`vgs` did not enumerate
`nfs-vg` during inspection. The legacy `/srv/nfs-lv` filesystem remained
mounted read-write and exported; that is **not evidence that its data or disk
is healthy**. The exact fault domain (disk, cable, power, or controller) is
unconfirmed. Do not try `drbdadm attach`, rerun initialization, format, repair,
or restart the NFS service as a response to this finding. Investigate the
physical I/O path and protect the legacy data under a reviewed incident plan
before attempting replica recovery. Consult
[the next-stage runbook](nfs-ha-next-stage.md#1-resolve-the-storage-incident).
